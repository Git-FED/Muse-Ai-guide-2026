# Community Guide

Join the Muse community on [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Muse_sub/).

## Share safely

- Share sanitized examples, not secrets or private learner data.
- Explain the goal, context, and constraints behind a result.
- Include what you verified.
- Be constructive when reviewing another person’s workflow.
- Label drafts, experiments, and confirmed results clearly.
