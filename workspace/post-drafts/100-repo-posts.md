# 100 Repo Posts

A structured draft bank for repository announcements, lesson sharing, community prompts, and Muse updates. Drafts are not publication approval.

## Rules

- Every draft needs a clear audience and purpose.
- Keep claims verifiable and dated when relevant.
- Include the intended URL and image status.
- Adapt wording for the destination.
- Mark each draft `DRAFT`, `READY FOR REVIEW`, or `APPROVED`.
- Do not publish directly from this file.
